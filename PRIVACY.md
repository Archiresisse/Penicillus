# Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

_Last updated: 2026-11-26_

Penicillus is a local desktop application for Windows. It is open-source software maintained by Archiresisse.
Penicillus 是在 Windows 上本機執行的桌面應用程式，為開源軟體。

## Google sign-in / Google 登入

Penicillus can offer "Sign in with Google". When you use it, the app requests only the basic scopes `openid`, `email` and `profile`, which give it your Google account identifier, email address and display name.
Penicillus 提供「使用 Google 登入」，僅請求 `openid`、`email`、`profile` 基本範圍，取得你的 Google 帳號識別碼、電子郵件與顯示名稱。

- This information is used only to identify you inside the app.
- It is stored on your own computer. It is not sent to the developer or to any third party, and it is not sold or shared.
- Penicillus does not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
- You can sign out in the app at any time, and you can revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

- 這些資訊只用來在應用程式內辨識你的身分。
- 資料保存在你自己的電腦上，不會傳送給開發者或任何第三方，也不會出售或分享。
- Penicillus 不會存取你的 Gmail、雲端硬碟、聯絡人或其他 Google 資料。
- 你可以隨時在應用程式內登出，也可以到 https://myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤銷授權。

## Other data / 其他資料

Images, prompts and projects you create stay on your computer. Penicillus does not collect analytics or telemetry.
你建立的圖片、提示詞與專案都保存在你的電腦上。Penicillus 不蒐集分析或遙測資料。
